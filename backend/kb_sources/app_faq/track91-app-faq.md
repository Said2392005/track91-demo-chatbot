## Q: How do I register a new GPS device?

Go to **Fleet > Devices > Add Device**, then enter the device's serial number (printed on the
device housing) and the IMEI number of its SIM card. Select the vehicle you want to link the
device to, or choose "Unassigned" to register it without linking yet. The device will show as
"Pending" until it sends its first location ping, then automatically switches to "Active."

## Q: How do I add a driver?

Go to **Fleet > Drivers > Add Driver** and fill in the driver's name, phone number, and license
number. License number is optional but recommended, since some reports group driving behavior
by license number for drivers who move between vehicles. Once saved, the driver appears in the
driver list and can be assigned to any vehicle in your fleet.

## Q: How do I assign a driver to a vehicle?

Open **Fleet > Vehicles**, select the vehicle, and choose **Assign Driver** from the vehicle
detail panel. Only one driver can be the primary assignment on a vehicle at a time; assigning a
new driver replaces the previous assignment but does not delete the previous driver's trip or
alert history.

## Q: How do I generate a trip report?

Go to **Reports > Trips**, select a vehicle or a fleet group, and choose a date range. The
report shows each trip's start/end time, locations, distance, and duration, and can be exported
as PDF or CSV from the **Export** button in the top-right corner.

## Q: How do I export data as CSV?

Most report and list screens (Trips, Alerts, Maintenance, Vehicle Roster) have an **Export**
button in the top-right corner. Choose **CSV** from the export format dropdown; the file
downloads directly to your browser's default download location.

## Q: How do I add a new vehicle to my fleet?

Go to **Fleet > Vehicles > Add Vehicle** and enter the registration/plate number, make, model,
and year. You can optionally assign a fleet group and a GPS device at the same time, or add
those later from the vehicle's detail page.
