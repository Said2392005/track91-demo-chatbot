## Plans Overview

Track91 offers three standard plans, billed per vehicle per month: Starter, Pro, and
Enterprise. All plans include unlimited users, live tracking, and standard alert types. Plan
pricing below is the approved, publishable rate as of this version — this document is the only
source the chatbot may quote a price from.

## Starter Plan

The Starter plan costs ₹499 per vehicle per month (billed annually) or ₹599 per vehicle per
month (billed monthly). It includes live tracking, trip history for 3 months, and email alerts
for speeding and low fuel.

## Pro Plan

The Pro plan costs ₹899 per vehicle per month (billed annually) or ₹1,049 per vehicle per month
(billed monthly). It includes everything in Starter plus geofencing, driver behavior scoring,
maintenance scheduling reminders, and 12-month trip history.

## Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan starts at ₹1,499 per vehicle per month (billed annually) for fleets of 50+
vehicles, and includes everything in Pro plus API access, custom reporting, and a dedicated
account manager. Fleets requiring custom volume pricing above 500 vehicles, or non-standard
contract terms, should contact the sales team directly — this document does not cover
custom/negotiated Enterprise pricing.

## Add-ons & Discounts

A 10% discount applies to any plan when paying annually instead of monthly (already reflected
in the annual prices above). Additional GPS hardware devices can be purchased separately;
device pricing is quoted at the time of order and is not covered in this document.
