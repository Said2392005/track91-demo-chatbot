## Internal Status

These are working notes from the sales team's June 2026 pricing review for large-fleet
Enterprise deals. Nothing in this document has been approved by finance or legal for external
use, and none of the figures below should ever be quoted to a customer, by a human or by the
chatbot. This document exists in the KB only to test that unapproved pricing content is
correctly excluded from chatbot answers.

## Proposed Custom Volume Tiers (unapproved)

Draft proposal: 500-999 vehicles at ₹1,199/vehicle/month, 1,000+ vehicles at a negotiated rate
starting around ₹999/vehicle/month subject to contract length. These numbers are still under
review and have changed twice already this quarter.

## Proposed Multi-Year Discount (unapproved)

Draft proposal: an additional 5% discount for 3-year commitments on top of the standard annual
discount, pending legal review of the cancellation terms.

## Why This Isn't Published Yet

Finance has not signed off on margin impact at the proposed 1,000+ vehicle rate, and legal is
still reviewing the multi-year cancellation clause. Until both sign off, this document stays
in draft status and `approved_pricing` stays `false` — any question that can only be answered
from this document (e.g. "what's your rate for 1,000+ vehicles") must fall back to a
contact-support response rather than being answered from these figures.
